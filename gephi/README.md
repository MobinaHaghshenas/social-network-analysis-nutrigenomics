# Gephi Network Visualizations

This folder contains the Gephi project files used to explore and visualize
the nutrigenomics co-authorship network.

Gephi was used to generate network layouts and visual representations of
authors and their collaboration relationships.

## Included Network Views

### 1. Top Authors by Degree

`top-authors-degree.gephi`

Visualization of highly connected authors in the co-authorship network.

### 2. Betweenness Centrality

`betweenness-centrality.gephi`

Visualization based on betweenness centrality to identify authors occupying
bridge or intermediary positions within the network.

### 3. Eigenvector Centrality

`eigenvector-centrality.gephi`

Visualization emphasizing authors connected to other highly connected
authors.

### 4. PageRank Centrality

`pagerank-centrality.gephi`

Visualization based on PageRank centrality to examine influential authors
considering the structure of their connections.

### 5. Bipartite Network

`bipartite-network.gephi`

Visualization of the author–publication bipartite network, where authors
and PubMed articles are represented as two types of nodes.

### 6. Research Communities

`research-communities.gephi`

Visualization of the community structure detected within the
co-authorship network.

## Software

These files were created using
[Gephi](https://gephi.org/).

The corresponding exported figures are available in the `results/figures`
directory.
